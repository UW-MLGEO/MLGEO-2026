*Note that this assignment is for those students who are enrolled in 469.*

## Objective(s)

You will need to assemble a single multi-panel figure that explores one or more concepts that are central to your group’s ML application. 

*What* to represent is up to you and your groups. Questions to consider: 

- What kind of figures will help for your presentation and writeups? 
- What sort of information is fundamental to your specific application? 
- Are there data or concepts that you are especially interested in conveying? 

For this assignment:

- "Multi-panel" refers to a figure with more than two subfigures.
- You will need to upload to Github (in the ```/assignments/final/469/``` directory) <u>a single .PDF</u> with your figure on one page and caption on another. The top-left of each panel should have a letter in a box.

## Rubric

| Criterion | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Completeness** | Missing most instructions; single panel or no figure. | Missing several instructions; only two panels. | Follows most instructions; more than two panels but missing minor elements (e.g., panel labels). | Follows all instructions with minor issues. | Fully follows all instructions; multiple well-labeled panels submitted as a single PDF with a detailed caption. |
| **Coherency** | Panels are unrelated and lack any unifying theme. | Panels are loosely connected but the relationship may be unclear. | Panels share a general theme. | Panels share a clear theme with a logical narrative. | Panels tell a cohesive, well-structured story with a clear unifying concept. |
| **Clarity** | Figures are difficult to interpret; poor labeling, resolution, or layout. | Figures are somewhat readable but have significant issues with axes, labels, or formatting. | Figures are reasonably clear but could benefit from better annotation or layout. | Figures are clear and well-formatted with minor room for improvement. | Figures are immediately understandable with clean formatting, clear labels, and effective use of color and layout. |
| **Relevance** | No apparent connection to the ML application. | Somewhat related to the ML application. | Related to the ML application but may not address any central concepts. | Clearly connected to the ML application and addresses one or more critical aspects of the work. | Directly tied to central concepts of the ML application and provides meaningful insight. |
