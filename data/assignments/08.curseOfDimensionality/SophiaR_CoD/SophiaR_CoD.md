![Recreation of Figure 1: A histogram and scatter plot showing 1,000 randomly generated points drawn from a distribution with a mean of 0 and a standard deviation of 1. The plots highlight points that fall within one standard deviation of the values 1.5 and (1.5, 1.5)](SophiaR_Figure.png)
*Figure 1: Data sparsity is shown to occur in higher dimensions with only 7.4% of the scatter plot data occuring within 1σ of (1.5, 1.5) and 31.2% of the histogram data occuring within 1σ of 1.5.*

Interpretation: This data sparsity is a result of too many dimensions, which leads us to believe that data collection in this manner will not be representative of the total population.
